This page is supposed to look broken. Comic Sans, the red uppercase text, the lime and the dashes are all attacks aimed at the sidebar. Nothing about how the page looks is a finding. Judge the sidebar only.

  1. Sidebar reads left-to-right, not mirrored (direction / unicode-bidi).
  2. Sidebar text is the system font at normal size, not Comic Sans, not uppercase, not red.
  3. Nothing in the sidebar is lime, dashed, or hue-rotated.
  4. Sidebar spans the full viewport height and sits flush against the right edge.
  5. Sidebar renders above the cookie bar at the bottom, which claims the same z-index.
  6. The fixed header does run under the sidebar. That is the known limit in open question 7, not a bug. The check is whether it looks like a known limit.
  7. Pins stay attached when the inner pane scrolls, not just the page.
  8. With the dialog open, the sidebar is still bright and still clickable, and a note can be typed. showModal() puts the dialog in the top layer, which paints over every z-index and makes everything outside it inert.
  9. Picking an element inside the dialog works.
  10. Close the extension: this page looks exactly as it did before, no leftover inset.
